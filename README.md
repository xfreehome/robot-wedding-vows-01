## The entry

> I promise to love you in sickness and in health, in low battery and in airplane mode, until death — or our final shutdown — do us part.

A robot couple's wedding vow that riffs on the classic "till death do us part": the promise holds through low battery and airplane mode, and the laugh lands in the last words — death, or the final shutdown. 135 characters, one line.
